Privacy

Last updated: October 2026. Review this page before launch and adjust it to match how you run the service.

Guest notes

Without an account, notes are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Account notes

When you sign in, we store your email address and your notes in our database, hosted by Supabase. Notes are private to your account unless you turn on link sharing for a note. Anyone with a shared link can read that note until you turn sharing off or delete the note.

The assistant

If you use the assistant, the text of the open note and your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce an answer. We keep a daily count of assistant messages per account to limit use. We don't store the conversation.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or show ads based on your notes.
  • We don't read your private notes, except where required by law or to investigate a reported shared note.

Deleting your data

Delete any note from your notes list at any time. To delete your account and everything in it, contact us at the address below.
