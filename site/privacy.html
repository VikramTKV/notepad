Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Quillnote is run by . We collect as little as we can: no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no emails.

Notes you write without an account

Guest notes are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them, and we can't recover them.

If you create an account

We store:

  • your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, used only to sign you in;
  • your notes and their settings, such as whether a note is pinned or shared;
  • for shared notes, the link's expiry time and, if you set one, a hashed form of the share password. The share password itself is never stored.

This data is held by our database provider, Supabase, in its Sydney, Australia region. Each person can only reach their own notes; this is enforced by the database itself, not just by the website.

We don't send any emails, so we don't use your address for newsletters or marketing.

Shared links

When you turn on sharing for a note, anyone with its link can read that note until you turn sharing off, the link expires, or you delete the note. Treat a share link like the note itself. Shared notes show only the note's title and contents, never your email address.

The writing assistant

If the assistant is switched on and you use it, the text of the open note and your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude service to produce an answer. We keep a daily count of assistant messages per account to limit use. We don't store the conversation.

Website hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as IP addresses to keep its service secure; see GitHub's own privacy statement. Our fonts and code are hosted with the site itself, so loading the page doesn't contact any other company.

What we keep in your browser

Besides guest notes, your browser keeps your sign-in session and a few preferences: theme, text style, sort order, whether the notes list is shown, and any account changes waiting to sync while you're offline. These are needed for the site to work and are never used to track you.

How long we keep data, and your choices

  • See or take your data: every note can be downloaded from its ⋯ menu.
  • Correct it: edit any note at any time.
  • Delete it: delete single notes, or choose Delete account in the account menu to permanently remove your account and every note in it.

We keep account data until you delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object or to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Quillnote isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
