Questions

Is Notelume free?

Yes. Writing, saving, accounts and sharing are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, notes are kept in this browser on this device. An account keeps them safe if you clear your browser and lets you open them anywhere.

Who can read my notes?

Only you. Account notes are protected in the database so each person can reach only their own. A note becomes readable by others only when you turn on its share link.

How do password-protected links work?

When you add a password to a shared note, the password is scrambled before it's stored, and readers must type it before the note's contents are sent to them. Turn the link off at any time and it stops working straight away.

What happens if I forget my password?

We don't send emails, so passwords can't be reset by email. Keep yours somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once you've opened the notepad, it loads without a connection. Guest notes save normally; account notes are held on your device and sent as soon as you're back online.

Can I get my notes out?

Any note downloads as plain text, Markdown, a Word document or a web page, or prints to PDF.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account menu at the top right of the notepad and choose Delete account. Your account and every note in it are removed for good.
